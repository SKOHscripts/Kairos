Kairos tells you what to do now. It puts your tasks in the order to do them, fits them into the gaps of your day around your meetings, learns how long they really take and helps you anchor your habits. You capture, Kairos decides. No account, no ads: your data stays on your device.

<b>At a glance</b>
<ul>
<li><b>A list that sorts itself</b>: the task to do now is at the top, and Kairos explains why.</li>
<li><b>A day that holds</b>: tasks around your meetings, deep work protected, lighter work in the afternoon dip.</li>
<li><b>Habits that take hold</b>: a stable hour, an “After…” phrase, a validated question, scientific sources.</li>
<li><b>Durations that get sharper</b>: on-device machine learning learns how long your tasks really take.</li>
<li><b>The time really spent</b>: a timer per task, alerts and honest statistics.</li>
<li><b>Your data with you</b>: no account, no trackers, offline by default.</li>
</ul>

<b>An order you can explain</b>
Each task gets a priority score (WSJF): its value (priority P0, P1 or P2, plus the urgency of its deadline) divided by its effort in points. Small and important goes before big and distant; an overdue task always comes first. “Why this position?” shows the calculation in one tap.

<b>A realistic day</b>
Fixed times, recurring tasks (daily, weekdays, weekly, “on the 23rd of the month”), dependencies, subtasks, public holidays of 13 countries. A “Your day” card sums up tasks, deadlines, real availability and deep work.

<b>Habits, on a scientific basis</b>
Turn a recurring task into a habit. Kairos suggests the time you already do it, then never moves it: a stable cue is what builds automaticity. An “After my coffee” phrase ties it to your routine. Now and then, when you tick it, a validated question asks whether you do it automatically, and a curve shows where you stand. No guilt-tripping streak: a single miss does not count. Every claim is sourced, in the app and on the website.

<b>On-device machine learning, not AI</b>
A model trained on your device gives a new task a duration from your finished tasks that look like it, with a range. Kairos only applies it once it beats the default duration on your own history. No chatbot, no cloud.

<b>Capture without friction (GTD)</b>
An idea? A note. A task? A title is enough: it waits in “To process” for a priority and a size, one tap each.

<b>Real time</b>
A timer per task with an ongoing notification; alerts when you go past the estimate, forget a running timer or need a break, even with the app closed. When you tick a task, Kairos asks the time spent; interruptions are reported after the fact.

<b>Manage a team (optional)</b>
A second space for the manager, the only user: members, backlog, each person’s load, Monte Carlo forecasts and file exchanges with members.

<b>Your data stays with you</b>
No account, no ads, no trackers. JSON export, an up-to-date copy in a folder you sync, calendars from .ics, ICS or CalDAV, read only. The network is used only by the optional online connections, off by default.

Also for Windows, Linux, macOS and the browser, in Material or “Cadran” style. Free software (GNU AGPL v3 or later), in English, French, Spanish, German, Russian and Italian.
