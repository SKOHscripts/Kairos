Kairos 4.4: connect a CalDAV server (Nextcloud, Radicale, Fastmail…) on Android and desktop, read-only, and subscribe to an ICS link in the web version too. Public holidays of 13 countries are built in, the device's by default, and an imported calendar can count its whole-day events as holidays: closures, school holidays, regional holidays.
