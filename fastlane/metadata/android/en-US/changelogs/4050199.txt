Kairos 4.5: working time. When you tick a task, Kairos asks how long it took, lets you review tasks without a duration and chains on with “Done, next”. Report an interruption after the fact: you get statistics, and expected interruptions count in your availability, timeline and forecast. A “Your day” card tops the Day view with tasks, deadlines, real availability and deep-work advice. A deleted “day N of the month” task no longer comes back.
