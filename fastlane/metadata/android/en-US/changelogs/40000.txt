Kairos 4.0: your devices stay in sync. Link the same file (Syncthing, Nextcloud…) on each one: Kairos merges the changes, with no account or server, and shares what it learned about your durations. An on-device machine-learning model learns from your finished tasks how long they really take. A type is now needed to qualify a task: tasks without one come back to “To process”. Plus many fixes, especially for teams.
