The right time for every task

Kairos puts your tasks in the order to do them and fits them into the gaps of your day, around your meetings. No account, no ads, no network: your data stays on your device.

What Kairos does

An order you can explain

score = (value + urgency) ÷ effort
Priority and deadline make the value, points the effort. Small and important goes before big and distant; an overdue task always comes first.

A realistic day

Tasks placed around meetings, protected deep-work slots, lighter tasks during the afternoon dip, recurrences, dependencies and public holidays.

Real time

A timer per task, alerts when you go past the estimate, forget a timer or need a break, and statistics comparing your estimates with the time actually spent.

Preview

First installation

  • macOS: the app is not notarised by Apple. The first time, right-click Kairos, choose “Open” and confirm.
  • Windows: SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC”: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  • Android: allow installation from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
  • Coming from Kairos 2: your data is brought over at first launch (automatically on Android, on request on desktop).
